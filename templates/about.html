About RealHome

Connecting people with homes they love, across Bangalore and beyond.

Who We Are

Founded in 2025, RealHome is a modern real estate platform designed to make buying, selling, and renting homes effortless. Our goal is to bring transparency, simplicity, and trust to the real estate market in Bangalore.

We provide verified listings, personalized filters, and smart search tools to help you find the perfect property faster. From cozy apartments to luxurious villas, RealHome curates listings that fit every lifestyle and budget.

Our passionate team is dedicated to creating a seamless property experience for every user, combining technology with local expertise.

Our Mission

At RealHome, our mission is to redefine how people find their homes. We strive to make every property search smart, secure, and enjoyable — empowering people with the tools and information to make confident decisions.
